We Highlights

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang esports highlights

We Highlights is a small channel that cuts the best moments out of Mobile Legends: Bang Bang esports broadcasts and shares them as short highlight videos on TikTok and YouTube.

The We Highlights uploader

The uploader is a small program that the channel owner runs on their own computer. It is not a public service and visitors cannot sign up or log in to it.

What it does with TikTok:

  • The channel owner signs in with their own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and approves the app.
  • The app then sends the owner's finished highlight videos to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts (Content Posting API, video.upload).
  • The owner opens the draft in the TikTok app, writes the caption and posts it themselves. The app does not post anything on its own.

It only ever works with the owner's own account and the owner's own videos. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and the Terms of Service for the rules.
