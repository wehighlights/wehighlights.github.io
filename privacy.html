Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy covers the We Highlights website and the We Highlights uploader, a small program the channel owner runs on their own computer to send the channel's own videos to TikTok. In short: the uploader only touches the owner's own TikTok account, keeps its login on the owner's computer, and shares nothing with anyone.

Who we are

We Highlights is a Mobile Legends: Bang Bang esports highlights channel run by one person (MINN). Contact: wehighlights22@gmail.com.

What the uploader accesses on TikTok

The uploader uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. When the owner signs in and approves it, it can use:

  • user.info.basic: the account's basic profile information (open ID, display name and avatar), which TikTok returns when signing in.
  • video.upload: permission to send a video to the account's TikTok inbox as a draft. The owner finishes and posts it in the TikTok app.

The uploader is used only with the owner's own account. It does not read other people's accounts, followers, messages or videos.

What is stored, and where

  • The sign-in tokens TikTok issues (an access token, valid for 24 hours, and a refresh token, valid for up to 365 days) are saved in a file on the owner's own computer.
  • The app's client key and client secret are saved in a file on the same computer.
  • Nothing is stored on a server or in a database, and nothing is kept about website visitors by us.

Who receives data

The only party the uploader sends data to is TikTok, to sign in and to deliver the video to the owner's inbox. We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

This website

This website is a few static pages. We do not run analytics, use cookies or collect any information from visitors. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs under its own privacy statement.

Deleting data and removing access

  • The owner can delete the saved sign-in by deleting the token file on their computer.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time in the TikTok app, in the settings list of apps that are connected to the account. Tokens also expire on their own.

Children

The uploader and this website are not meant for children and do not knowingly collect information from them.

Third-party content

Match footage in our videos comes from esports broadcasts and belongs to its owners. This policy is about personal data only; see the Terms of Service for content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
